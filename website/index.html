FAQ

Questions.

Is Margin free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no account. The optional online AI uses your own NVIDIA API key.

Does the AI work offline?

Yes. Download the 1.1 GB on-device model once from Settings, and questions are answered on your device with nothing sent anywhere.

Where do my notes go?

They stay on your device, and in your own iCloud Drive folder or Google backup if you use one. If you add an NVIDIA key, your question and the notes it needs are sent to NVIDIA to answer it.

Do I need a stylus?

No. You can write with a finger. With a stylus you also get pressure, optional tilt and palm rejection.

Are the iPad and Android apps the same?

The core is: ink, PDFs, recording and the study tools. The iPad app is further along, with live transcripts, Live Text, iCloud sync, more paper styles and tools such as signatures and study tape. Notes don't move between the two yet.
